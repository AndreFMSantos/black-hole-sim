# O3 — DOP853 on the double path, and the three bugs it uncovered

Plan: plan-refinement-phase. Previous: [32-step-control](32-step-control.md).

The plan was a faster integrator for the double path. Comparing two *independent* integrators at tight tolerances also exposed three real defects: two in the Schwarzschild-de Sitter path and one in the velocity reprojection that affected every metric, and float most of all.

## 1. DOP853 (order 8) for the pure-double thin-disk/sky path
- **Tableau.** Generated by `tools/gen_dop853.py` from SciPy 1.18.1 `dop853_coefficients.py`, itself from Hairer's `dop853.f`, with 17 significant digits. Nothing was typed by hand. The header is `apps/v4_0-metric-api/dop853_coeffs.cuh`.
- **Error norm.** Hairer's combination of the order-5 and order-3 estimators, applied per component with the same weights as DP45 and a max norm: err_i = h·e5²/√(e5² + 0.01·e3²)/w_i. The step factor is 0.9 err^{-1/8}, clamped to [1/3, 6].
- **Disk crossing.** The cubic Hermite (O(h⁴)) would limit the longer DOP853 steps, so the bisection on the Hermite is followed by one Newton correction with real DOP853 sub-steps from y.
- **Scope:**
  - only `S = F = double` without a volume, via `if constexpr`;
  - volume models keep DP45, because their sampling relies on short Hermite segments;
  - float keeps DP45, which is the right order at tol 2e-5.
- **Switch and default.** `--integrator dop853|dp45`, default dop853. The equivalence selftests against the CPU tracer (the same DP45 algorithm) pin dp45.
- **Calibration (rule 2).** Tolerances of different methods are not comparable. DOP853 runs at tol/10, and at that setting **every** quantile (max, p99, median) of the sky direction, disk r and disk φ is no worse than DP45 at the same `--tol`. At plain tol it was 1.6× faster but the sky median was 2.3× worse.
- **The O2 scaling (h·r on the way out) is applied at any tolerance here.** Order-8 steps are long, and without it 38 % of the attempts were rejected (with it, 4 %).

## 2. Bug: Schwarzschild-de Sitter initial radial velocity (since v2.1c)
- **Symptom.** In SdS the two tight references (DP45 tol 1e-12, DOP853 tol 1e-12) differed by 1e-2 rad. DP45 got *worse* as its tolerance was tightened (1e-9: right; 1e-10: 4e-4; 1e-13: 2e-3 off). DOP853 converged.
- **Isolation:**
  - Forcing small steps (a temporary h cap) made both integrators drift identically, so the cause was something done per step and not the tableau.
  - Schwarzschild did not drift (6e-11 between 97 and 5120 steps); SdS did.
  - Without the radial reprojection, the SdS ray was captured after 15 steps, while the Schwarzschild ray was unchanged.
- **Cause.** `S y[6] = {S(r0), S(-delta_d(r0, a, q2) * in.pr), ...}`: Δ without the −Λr⁴/3 term, so the initial dr/dτ was wrong. The reprojection onto √R fixed its magnitude after each step, so the error stayed small but depended on the step sizes.
- **Fix.** `delta_d(r0, a, q2, lam)`. Now DP45 1e-8 … 1e-13 and DOP853 converge to the same μ = −0.2647949364 within 1e-12. The previously "stable" value, −0.26205, was 2.7e-3 rad off.
- **Size of the old error** (float, against the tight reference):
  - the SdS disk golden scene had 270 rays out of 36 864 with a wrong fate, sky errors up to 7e-2 rad (4°) and disk r p99 1.9e-2;
  - now there are 2 boundary rays and p99 5e-5 rad.
  - The SdS shadow-size test always passed, because the fates on the shadow edge barely depend on it.
- **Frozen versions (updated 2026-11-19):** the SdS initial-velocity + exit interpolation + `r_c` step cap fix has now been **backported** to both `v2_x-variants-cuda` (via `tools/refactor/extract_versions.sh`) and `v3_1-kerr-cuda-ui`; the conditional turning-points velocity reprojection (§4) has been backported to all frozen CUDA versions (`v0_6b`–`v3_1`).

## 3. Bug: SdS exit not interpolated
- The SdS sky direction is the position at r = 0.9999 r_c, but the code took the first accepted state *beyond* it. With long steps the overshoot changed the answer.
- The crossing of r_far_eff is now found on the Hermite (plus a Newton sub-step with DOP853).
- Each outgoing step in SdS is also capped at half the remaining distance to r_c, so no step jumps over the cosmological horizon.
- Both blocks are compiled only for the SdS and generic metric tags. Compiled into the Kerr kernels they cost 12–16 % in the float torus and RIAF kernels.

## 4. Bug: velocity reprojection at turning points (all metrics)
- After every step the velocities were set to ±√R(r) and ±√M(μ) (v0.6a, to keep the invariants). **Near a turning point this is ill-conditioned:** a position error δr becomes a velocity error ~√(R′δr), and through the sign a jump in Mino time.
- **Found on one SdS ray** whose step ended exactly on the periapsis (dr/dτ = +8e-6, R = 5e-10). It came out 1.1e-6 rad off at internal tol 1e-9, against ~1e-10 for its neighbours.
- **Fix.** Project only where it is well conditioned, y′² ≥ 200·|f′|·(atol + rtol·|x|), where f′/2 is k7, the derivative at the end of the step, which costs nothing. Near a turning point the second-order equation carries the velocity through.
  - **The μ projection keeps a pole guard** (σ < 0.1: always project). Without it, float rays along the axis got stuck at max_steps (selftest_axis failed).
  - **Threshold.** 100× the amplification (k7-based: 200|k7|). Values of 10 and 1000 measured within 2–4× of it; the step count is unchanged.

## Tests
- `v4_0_dop853` (`tests/dop853.py`): Kerr, KN, a far camera and SdS. The independent references (DOP853 1e-13 vs DP45 1e-11) agree within 1e-8 (measured ≤ 4e-9); DOP853 is no worse than DP45 in every quantile and takes fewer steps.
- `escape_tail` and `step_control` pass unchanged.
- **Golden images.** 12 cases moved to `images_v4/`:
  - 1-level changes in a few pixels of 8 double images;
  - both images of the two SdS cases (physics fix);
  - the float images are closer to double than before (e.g. `kerr-nt-sky-float` MAE 0.051 → 0.019).
- **Sweep.** `sweep_params --quick` flagged 2 of 485, the two long-known benign cases (an infall-observer isolated saturated pixel; KN dist 1.5 "terminate" message).
- **Overall.** ctest 37/37 serial.
